02 — The method

Three rules we never bend.

  1. 01

    A rule needs a page number.

    Rules are compiled from your brand-guideline PDF, with the exact line quoted. If the quote can’t be found on that page, the rule is thrown out. Nothing is enforced until a brand manager approves it.

  2. 02

    A number needs an owner.

    Every figure in your copy is matched to an approved fact with a named owner and an expiry date. It’s verified, in conflict, stale, or unverified. And unverified isn’t false. We say exactly that.

  3. 03

    The model proposes. Code decides.

    AI is limited to tone, it’s labelled, and it never blocks a deterministic finding. Any suggested fix is re-run through every check and accepted only if it removes a problem without creating a new one.

03 — The chain of custody

Three roles. One record.

Permissions are enforced on the server, not the screen. Every step lands in the audit trail.

Brand manager

Owns the truth.

Compiles rules from the guidelines, approves them, and maintains the facts the whole team checks against.

Creator

Ships with proof.

Checks copy before it leaves the desk, fixes what’s flagged, and submits it for review with the evidence attached.

Reviewer

Makes the call.

Sees every finding and its source, then records one decision.
